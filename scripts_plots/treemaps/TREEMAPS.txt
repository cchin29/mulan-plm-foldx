Composition of the benchmark subsets, under four grouping units.

Every view holds the mutations fixed and changes only what a group is. Going down the
list is the leakage ladder: the stricter the split, the coarser the unit it must hold out
whole, and the fewer independent units the benchmark actually contains.

Finer than the sequence clustering rather than coarser, because single-linkage
merges aggressively through shared inhibitor chains while the both-partners rule
keeps distinct antigen/antibody pairings apart. Definition-sensitive, and the
strictest rung only in the sense that it controls a different axis.

Dataset & methodology · Metric

Pooled Pearson vs per-structure Spearman

Per-structure Spearman answers the design question (rank mutations within a complex). Pooled Pearson is inflated by the spread between complexes — exactly where homology leakage hides. Smaller numbers = the evaluation getting stricter, not the model getting worse.

Interaction sites · Interpretation

Attention-interface localization vs ΔΔG accuracy

ESM-C 6B is the best ΔΔG arm yet near-worst at localizing interfaces — ~0.19 below its own 600M sibling. The two are different read-outs of the same head.
  • Mechanism: large PLMs delocalize — a rich pooled vector wins ΔΔG, so the head no longer needs to concentrate attention at the interface (no localization term in the loss).
  • Distinct from ESM3 (massive-activation outliers, below chance) — ESM-C 6B's embeddings are healthy; its drop is delocalization, not an artifact.
  • Takeaway: "attention recovers interfaces" is Ankh-specific, not a property that scales — worth flagging wherever the paper's interpretability claim is cited.

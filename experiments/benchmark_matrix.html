ProSST/ProMIM/ESM2 numbers are from ProtBFF Table 2 (same CD-HIT 60% split; dataset = SKEMPI2 minus the 10 largest complexes ≈ 6,631 muts vs MuLAN's 4,165 single-point — close, not identical). The S1102 clustered numbers (~0.65 P) are higher only because S1102 is a curated subset from the original MuLAN project, and an easier benchmark than full SKEMPI. No frontier paper reports per-structure Spearman on a sequence-clustered split.
AUROC — sign-of-effect (destabilizing vs not), the frontier's classification metric. The clean comparison is CATH (both all-muts), where MuLAN is highly competitive: its FoldX arm (ESM-C 6B + FoldX scalar) 0.791 is 2nd, above CATH-ddG; its best single arm reaches 0.806 (Ankh-large base).
